The Catskills, New York

Why Roxbury

A walkable Catskills village with real bones — Main Street, mountains, and room to build the kind of life that's getting harder to find within a few hours of the city.

Why here

A Catskills town on the rise

More people are choosing the Catskills for good. Remote and hybrid work have widened where life can happen; the trails, rivers, and ski slopes are a draw year-round; and towns like Roxbury still offer space and quiet within reach of the Hudson Valley and New York City. It's a place people are putting down roots, not just passing through.

Roxbury sits in Delaware County, in the western Catskills, among towns drawing a new wave of residents and visitors — Kenoza Lake, Roscoe, Livingston Manor, and Andes among them. It's a region with momentum and a strong sense of place.

The setting

A place, not just a parcel

Carriage Hill sits right at the village edge — minutes from Main Street, with town water and sewer nearby and road access already in place. These aren't isolated lots deep in the woods; they're part of a real community, with neighbors, services, and a town around them.
