The Catskills, New York

Why Roxbury

A walkable Catskills village with real bones — Main Street, mountains, and room to build, within a few hours of New York City.

The region

The western Catskills

Remote and hybrid work have widened where life can happen, and the trails, rivers, and ski slopes draw visitors year-round. Towns like Roxbury still offer space and quiet within a few hours of New York City.

Roxbury sits in Delaware County, near Kenoza Lake, Roscoe, Livingston Manor, and Andes.

The setting

A place, not just a parcel

Carriage Hill sits at the village edge — minutes from Main Street, with town water and sewer nearby and road access in place. The parcels sit within the community, not off in the woods.
