Why Roxbury

Roxbury is a walkable Catskills village with a proper Main Street and nearby ski mountains within a few hours of New York City.

The western Catskills

Remote and hybrid work have widened where life can happen, and the trails, rivers, and ski slopes draw visitors year-round. Towns like Roxbury still offer space and quiet within a few hours of New York City.

Roxbury sits in Delaware County, near Kenoza Lake, Roscoe, Livingston Manor, and Andes.

Plattekill and Belleayre

Two ski mountains sit within easy reach. Plattekill Mountain is minutes from the village, a family-run mountain known for its glades and uncrowded weekends. Belleayre Mountain, a larger state-run resort, is about forty minutes away. In the warmer months the same slopes turn to mountain biking and hiking.

A place, not just a parcel

Carriage Hill sits at the village edge, minutes from Main Street. Town water and sewer are nearby, and road access is in place. The parcels sit within the community, not off in the woods.
